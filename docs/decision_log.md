# Decision log

## Purpose and how to read this document

This log records, in chronological order, every methodological decision taken in the project that tests global marine habitat classifications with OBIS fauna records, together with its rationale and the key numerical results. It is information that will not go in the paper, but that is needed to understand and reproduce the choices behind the code.

The classifications tested are:

- **EBU** (Ecological Benthic Units; Harris et al. 2026), with groups A (shelf), B (slope), C (seamounts), D (ridges and rifts), E (abyssal and hadal) and F (plateaus), and 57 regions;
- **EMU** (Ecological Marine Units; Sayre et al. 2017), 37 classes, three-dimensional.

They are tested at global level 1 (25 km EASE-Grid 2.0) and in 8 regional windows at level 2. The project then derives environmental gradients and thresholds from GLORYS12 (Gradient Forest, GDM), builds new habitats independent of EBU and EMU, and finally produces a united top-down x bottom-up map.

Work was organised in phases and in decision points called **Gates** (Gate 1 to Gate 9), at which the PI decided item by item. Each section below follows that order. Tables list the decision (Item | Decision | Rationale where the log gives one); bullets give results.

Conventions:

- The project values **effect size** (omega-squared, written omega² or R²) over p-values: with this much data almost everything is significant, so results are read by effect size first (see Gate 4).
- Identifiers (script names, file names, column names, category values) are in Portuguese because they are code. They are kept exactly as in the repository, with a short English gloss in parentheses at first use. See `docs/glossary.md` for the Portuguese-English glossary.
- Paths are repository-relative. `saida/...` is the "outputs/" folder of the working tree.
- Dates are written dd/mm/yyyy (all in 2026). Numbers use English separators: comma for thousands (7,117 files) and point for decimals (0.8).
- "Stand-by" is kept as a status: a habitat or class with a data gap, not an absence of habitat.

Terminology used in the log: unit = sampling unit; "distinct on its own / partly distinct" = whether a habitat is distinguished by the fauna by itself or only partly; "separates" = omega² >= 0.01 and power >= 80% (defined in Gate 4).

## 3. The 30% stop in level-1 curation (09/10/2026): decisions approved by the PI

The curation pipeline was set to stop when more than 30% of records were lost by a given check in a taxonomic group. The problems found and the decisions:

| Problem | Decision |
|---|---|
| Dates such as "2007-09/ 2007-10" with no `date_year` in OBIS (CPR: 1.3 million records) | Year, start and end read from the original `eventDate` when OBIS did not interpret it; the year is valid if start and end are in the same year. Column `data_relida` (date re-read) |

| Duplicates between datasets were removing replicates within the same dataset | Rule corrected: a duplicate is only the same occurrence in different datasets; the dataset with more metadata is kept |
| Absences above 30% in Echinodermata and Anthozoa | Expected (MLPA kelp monitoring); no change |
| Date with no `eventDate` (CPR: year, month and day only in separate fields) | Date read in order: year/month/day fields; ISO eventDate; other formats for the year only. Extraction redone with year_txt, month_txt, day_txt and verbatimEventDate |

## 8. Phase 8, stage 1 at level 1 (17/10/2026, technical design; awaited Gate 4)

- Scripts: `scripts/81_etapa1_n1.R`, `scripts/funcoes_permanova.R`; outputs `saida/etapa1_n1_permanova.csv`, `saida/etapa1_n1_indval.csv`.
- Sampling unit: 25 km cell (benthos) or cell x EMU (pelagic), purity >= 0.8, >= 10 events; a class needs >= 5 units. Rarefaction: 10 events drawn per unit, 5 draws.
- Dissimilarity: sqrt(beta-sim) (main) and sqrt(Sorensen) (support); the square root avoids negative sums of squares (beta-sim is not Euclidean).
- A trial showed that permuting only within blocks with D ~ class gives p ~ 1 by construction when blocks have a single class. Design adopted: (a) global, D ~ class, free permutation (descriptive); (b) main test, D ~ block + class, permutations within 2,500 km blocks, only blocks with >= 2 classes.
- Own PERMANOVA implementation (same statistic as `adonis2`, checked: identical R², F and p), about 65x faster; 14 parallel processes. GPU not used (the packages are CPU-only).
- IndVal.g on the units of (b), 9,999 restricted permutations, Holm and BH p-values.
- Environment: Rtools45 (rtools45-6768-6492, valid signature) installed on 19/10/2026 with the PI's authorisation; gradientForest 0.1.37 and extendedForest 1.6.2 compiled from the mirror github.com/r-forge/gradientforest (`scripts/80b_gradientforest.R`).

## 19. Gate 9 (30/10/2026), decided by the PI: criteria 1 to 8 accepted (final map uniting top-down and bottom-up)

**Principle:** separate when any evidence separates; merge only when the fauna shows equivalence with power (precaution; heterogeneity matters for conservation). Script: `scripts/91_mapa_unido_bentos.R`.

| Criterion | Decision |
|---|---|
| 1 Candidates | Crossing of depth band x new habitat (HB) x EBU region in each cell |
| 2 Merge | Only by fauna equivalence (stage-2 criterion, consensus >= 2/3 of >= 2 groups, complete linkage, cut at 1/3); an untested pair is not merged |
| 3 Protected features | EBU groups C (seamounts) and D (ridges and rifts) merge only with the same group; hadal not yet separable at the region level |
| 4 Hierarchy | I compartment; II depth band (0-200, 200-2,000, > 2,000 m); III HB; IV EBU region; merge only within the band |
| 5 Minimum area | < 10 cells of 25 km, not protected and with no distinct pair -> nearest neighbouring class of the same band in GF space |
| 6 Attributes | Evidence (distinct / merged / tested with no conclusive pair / not tested = hatched), protected, shelf seasonality (annual amplitude of bottom temperature: strong >= 5 degC, moderate >= 2 degC) |

## 21. Known limitations and open issues

- **0.3:** measure the collection gear in the eMoF extension.
- **4.9:** check of the OBIS bathymetry against GEBCO on a sample (GEBCO not yet downloaded). Related: GLORYS depth is used as `prof` in training and prediction (Gate 7); GEBCO remains pending.
- **EBU discrepancy:** 591 `ebu_type` combinations in the data versus 250 EBUs in the paper (234 dominate some cell at 25 km).
- **Infauna / sediment:** the EBU (geomorphological) represents infauna poorly; sediment data are a pending item (Gate 5).
- **Pelagic:** few fitted taxa in the Gradient Forest (4-23); new pelagic habitats remain below the EMU in omega²; Euphausiacea and Hyperiidea show no signal and are out of the pelagic validation. The pelagic moves to level 2 (Gate 8, item 3) and the united pelagic map comes after level 2.
- **Benthos new habitats:** the bottom-salinity-free GF is the main model; the k-means weights by area and the abyssal tends to dominate (addressed by depth-band clustering, Gate 8).
- **Transfer of thresholds between basins:** whether thresholds found where data exist repeat in other basins is being tested in `scripts/88c_transferencia.R` (Gate 8, item 6).
- **Dynamism:** benthos treated as static (Gate 8b); shelf seasonality kept as an attribute; re-evaluate at level 2 with dense series after stage 4.
- **Stage 4** (monthly maps, area of applicability) not yet done.
- **Level 2:** EMU not testable inside the windows; Amazon mouth only a descriptive case study with no local validation; the 1,288 dateless records there are almost all "North BR Mangrove".
- **Environmental explanation of distinctions:** the AUC of GF distance for distinct versus non-distinct pairs is only 0.52-0.64, so the environment explains only part of the distinctions between habitats.
- **Cross-ocean transfer:** at level 1 the leave-one-block-out accuracy is low (EBU region 19%, EMU 18%; kappa 0.02-0.15): the fauna does not recognise the same class in another ocean.
- **Dispersion:** classes also differ in dispersion (accepted with a caveat at Gate 4).
- **Chaetognatha and Euphausiacea seasonality:** not testable at level 1.
- **Basin covariate:** `scripts/86b_bacias.R` is an approximation by rules and connectivity, with a barrier at the Isthmus of Panama.
